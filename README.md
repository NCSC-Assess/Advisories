# NCSC CVE Advisories

Security advisories published by NCSC in its role as a CVE Numbering Authority (CNA).

Each advisory directory holds the published CVE Record (CVE Record Format 5.1) alongside a
human-readable advisory. This repository is the NCSC CNA distribution point: it is public, requires
no registration or login, and imposes no terms of use restricting general-purpose use of the
information.

Vulnerability reports are handled under the [NCSC Responsible Disclosure Policy](https://github.com/NCSC-Assess/Disclosure-Policy).

## Disputing a CVE Record

NCSC is the Adjudicator for CVE Records it assigns. If you believe a record is inaccurate — that
the issue is not a vulnerability, that the affected products or versions are wrong, or that the
assignment itself is incorrect — you may dispute it.

**To initiate or escalate a dispute**, open an issue at https://github.com/NCSC-Assess/Advisories/issues/new, or use the NCSC contact
details in the CVE Program [List of Partners](https://www.cve.org/PartnerInformation/ListofPartners).
Document your rationale and include supporting evidence.

We acknowledge receipt in writing within **three business days** and decide within a further
**five business days**, notifying you if more time is required. Decisions may be escalated to our
Root and onward to the Top-Level Root, whose decision is final.

While a potentially legitimate dispute is under review, the record is tagged `disputed` with a
reason. If the dispute is found invalid, that tag is removed.

This follows the CVE Program's [Policy and Procedure for Disputing a CVE Record](https://www.cve.org/Resources/General/Policies/CVE-Record-Dispute-Policy.pdf).
